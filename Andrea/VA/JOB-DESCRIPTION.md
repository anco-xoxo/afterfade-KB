# Afterfade — Virtual Assistant Role

## Purpose of the role

**Keep day-to-day customer and commercial administration moving, while Andrea focuses on building and improving Afterfade's operations.**

The VA is the **first line of execution** for routine activities. This role is not a general assistant — it is a **Customer Service & Commercial Support VA** whose main purpose is to remove repetitive execution from Andrea.

### Core Principle

> **The VA handles the inbox, routine customer interactions and commercial administration. Andrea owns operations, systems, process improvement, escalations and coordination.**

---

## 1. Customer Service — PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY

The VA owns the routine customer inbox across:
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Web chat

### Daily Responsibilities

- Answer routine customer questions using the Afterfade knowledge base/SOPs.
- Help customers choose the appropriate product or kit.
- Ask customers for photos/information when needed.
- Handle basic order-status questions.
- Check tracking information.
- Communicate delivery updates.
- Follow up with customers when information is missing.
- Handle routine return requests by following the approved process.
- Collect information for damaged/lost parcels.
- Create/log tasks when follow-up is required.
- Keep conversations moving until they require escalation.

### Escalation Triggers

The VA should **not improvise answers** when the knowledge base doesn't cover something. Escalate:

- Complaints
- Refund decisions
- Complex replacements
- Coverage/quantity questions requiring a precise estimate
- Unusual surface/material questions
- Wholesale/B2B enquiries
- Professional painting/service quotes
- Customs problems
- Manufacturer/sourcing questions
- Discounts
- Anything operationally unclear

---

## 2. Order & Delivery Administration

The VA performs **routine checks** rather than Andrea doing them manually.

### VA Handles

- Look up orders in Sendcloud.
- Check shipment status.
- Retrieve tracking information.
- Identify basic delivery problems.
- Communicate tracking updates to customers.
- Collect information for carrier claims.
- Prepare/log shipping issues for escalation.
- Ensure relevant order numbers are attached to tasks.

### Invoices

- Locate/download invoices from Shopify when required.

**Current workflow:** Sendcloud = shipment/order status; Shopify = invoices.

---

## 3. Customer Service Task Management

The VA ensures **nothing falls through the cracks**.

### Task Flow

**Customer → Task → Owner → Status → Follow-up → Resolution**

The VA can:

- Create Trello/Vocero tasks.
- Add order numbers.
- Add customer information.
- Add screenshots/photos.
- Assign the task.
- Follow up on pending information.
- Update statuses.
- Close straightforward tasks once resolved.

**This is particularly important for Andrea.** Andrea should **not spend their day remembering which customer needs a follow-up**. The system should tell Andrea what actually requires operational attention.

---

## 4. Sales Support

The VA handles the **top of the sales funnel**, while Andrea focuses on the commercial/operational system behind it.

### VA Handles

- Responding to product enquiries.
- Helping customers understand the difference between products.
- Following up with interested customers.
- Collecting photos and project information.
- Identifying whether someone needs:
  - Complete Kit
  - Double Kit
  - Pro-Tech+ bottle
  - Professional service
- Passing qualified professional-service leads to the appropriate person.
- Following up on unanswered sales enquiries.

### Professional Service Enquiries

For professional painting/service enquiries, the VA collects:
- Photos
- Material
- What needs to be done
- Approximate number of windows/doors/etc.
- Condition
- Location

**The actual quote remains with the Afterfade team; the VA should never invent a price.**

---

## 5. Website Assistance

This is **administrative/content support**, not ownership of the website strategy.

### VA Can

- Identify broken/outdated information.
- Report FAQ inconsistencies.
- Collect recurring customer questions.
- Flag unclear product information.
- Check whether customer-facing information matches the current approved information.
- Prepare suggested FAQ/content updates for Andrea.
- Upload/change content **when the change has already been approved**.

**Andrea decides what the correct information should be. The VA helps execute the update.**

---

## 6. Social Media Support

The VA supports the operational side of social media:

- Monitor incoming messages/comments.
- Answer basic product questions.
- Forward leads into the sales/customer-service workflow.
- Collect interesting customer results.
- Organise UGC submissions.
- Create/log UGC tasks.
- Follow up with customers who have agreed to share content.
- Flag good before/after content for Andrea/marketing.

For UGC, the existing process requires consent and task tracking before the content is used. The VA **shouldn't own the brand/content strategy** unless that is explicitly assigned later.

---

## 7. What Andrea SHOULD NOT be doing anymore

This is the important part.

### Andrea Should Gradually Get Out Of

❌ Checking every routine tracking request
❌ Answering repetitive product questions
❌ Manually following up with customers
❌ Searching orders just to tell someone where their package is
❌ Creating every basic Trello card
❌ Chasing missing customer information
❌ Organising routine UGC submissions
❌ Doing repetitive sales follow-ups
❌ Monitoring every inbox personally
❌ Copy/pasting the same operational information repeatedly

### Andrea's Time Should Move Toward

#### Operations

**1. Process design**
- Build SOPs.
- Improve workflows.
- Identify bottlenecks.
- Define ownership.

**2. Systems**
- Shopify
- Sendcloud
- Trello/Vocero
- Inbox workflows
- Automations
- Notifications
- Data consistency

**3. Fulfilment & logistics**
- Work with the fulfilment centre.
- Investigate recurring shipping problems.
- Improve inventory visibility.
- Coordinate carrier processes.

**4. Inventory**
- Understand stock levels.
- Identify upcoming shortages.
- Coordinate replenishment.
- Create inventory procedures.

**5. Operational finance coordination**
- Supplier invoices.
- Large bills.
- Documentation.
- Coordination with accounting.

**6. Escalations**
- Difficult customers.
- Refund/complaint decisions.
- Complex delivery problems.
- B2B/wholesale.
- Exceptional cases.

**7. Management**
- Give Sebastiaan visibility on what is happening.
- Turn recurring problems into processes.
- Define KPIs.
- Identify what should be automated.
- Coordinate between customer service, sales, fulfilment and finance.

## In One Sentence

**The VA keeps customers, leads and routine administrative tasks moving; Andrea makes the machine itself run better.**

That distinction is important because otherwise the VA simply becomes **"another person doing customer service"**, while Andrea remains stuck doing the operational work *and* customer service.

Andrea's role should increasingly become **Operations Manager / Customer Operations**, with the VA acting as the execution layer underneath.

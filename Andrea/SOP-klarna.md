# SOP — Klarna Payment Questions

## Purpose
When customers ask about Klarna or report that Klarna isn't appearing at checkout, provide the steps below. Klarna is available on Afterfade, but customers must scroll past the express checkout buttons and fill in the standard form to see it.

## How Checkout Works

The Afterfade checkout page has two sections:

1. **Express checkout (top)** — buttons for Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc. These skip the standard form and go directly to that payment method. Klarna is **not** shown here.
2. **Standard checkout (below)** — customer enters email, shipping address, then sees all payment methods including Klarna.

If a customer clicks an express button, they will never see Klarna. They need to scroll past the express options and fill in the standard form instead.

## Response Template

### English
> Klarna is available as a payment option at Afterfade. Here's how to find it:
>
> 1. Add the product to your cart and go to checkout.
> 2. At the top of the checkout page you'll see **express checkout buttons** (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, etc.) — **skip these**.
> 3. Scroll down and fill in your **email and delivery address** in the standard form.
> 4. Continue to the **Payment** section — Klarna will appear alongside the other payment methods.
> 5. Select **Klarna** and complete your order.
>
> **Important:** Klarna only appears after filling in the standard checkout form. It is not available through the express buttons at the top.
>
> If Klarna still doesn't appear, send us a screenshot of the payment page and we'll look into it right away.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Likely cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Klarna not visible at checkout | Customer clicked an express button (Shop Pay, etc.) | Tell them to scroll past express buttons and fill in the standard form |
| Klarna not visible in standard form | Klarna eligibility check (soft credit check) — customer may not qualify, or region not supported | Ask for screenshot, escalate to team |
| Klarna option appears but payment fails | Klarna internal decline | Advise customer to contact Klarna support or try another method |

## Notes
- Klarna availability depends on customer country and credit status — it is not guaranteed for every buyer.
- Do not promise Klarna will always be available; say it "should" appear.
- If a customer sends a screenshot and Klarna is genuinely missing from the standard form, escalate to the team for Shopify/Klarna config review.

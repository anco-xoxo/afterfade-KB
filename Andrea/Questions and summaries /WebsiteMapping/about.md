About us

The Belgian company behind Afterfade
We help people bring faded aluminium and PVC back to life themselves, without a contractor and without replacing anything.

Afterfade Pro-Tech+ coating restoring an aluminium surface
What we make
Afterfade makes a two-part system for restoring faded aluminium and PVC. Prep+ strips away the grease, dirt and weathered film that build up over the years, and Pro-Tech+ is a clear coating that brings back the original colour and depth, then protects the surface for up to five years. It is not paint and it is not a wax. You apply it yourself in an afternoon, on window frames, doors, gates, garage doors and more.

Why it exists
Sun and UV strip the colour out of aluminium and PVC long before anything is actually wrong with them. The frames are still sound, they just look grey and tired, and the usual advice is to repaint or replace at a cost of thousands. We built Afterfade so people could fix the surface instead of the whole window.

Who we are
Afterfade is a registered Belgian company based in Paal, in Limburg. We ship across Europe from our own stock, we answer our own emails and WhatsApp messages, and we would rather talk someone out of a purchase than sell them a kit for a surface it will not work on. If you are unsure whether your frames are suitable, send us a photo before you order.

Shipping policy
1. Shipping area
We ship throughout Europe and to a number of countries outside Europe. The options available for your address, and the exact cost, are shown in checkout before you confirm your order.

2. Carriers
Orders are shipped with bpost, DPD or GLS via our logistics partner Sendcloud. Where available you can choose between home delivery and delivery to a pickup point during checkout.

3. Processing and delivery time
We aim to dispatch orders within 3 working days of receiving payment. You will receive a confirmation with track & trace as soon as your order ships. Delivery times are indicative and depend on the carrier and destination.

4. Shipping costs
Shipping costs are calculated and shown during checkout before you confirm your order.

Free shipping on orders of €75 or more to Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Austria, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and Greece. Below €75, home delivery costs €5,99 and a pickup point costs €3,95 where available.

Hungary, Ireland, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia and Sweden: €24,95 per order.
Cyprus and Malta: €39,95 per order.
All other countries: calculated in checkout based on destination and weight.

5. Risk
The risk of loss or damage passes to you as soon as you, or a third party designated by you, has physically received the goods.

6. Problems with delivery
Is your parcel late, lost or damaged? Contact us via hello@afterfade.be and we will resolve it with the carrier.

How it works
Three steps. One afternoon.
Spraying Afterfade Degreaser Prep+ to clean the surface
Step 1
Clean
Spray Prep+ over the faded surface and wipe away years of grime and chalking. Let it dry.

Afterfade Coating Pro-Tech+ dosed onto the applicator pad
Step 2
Coat
Drip Pro-Tech+ onto the applicator pad and glide it along the frame in thin, even strokes.

Buffing the coated frame to a satin finish
Step 3
Done
Buff lightly with the microfibre cloth. The colour is back, and stays protected for up to five years.

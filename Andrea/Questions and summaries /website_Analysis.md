## Fastest build order

To stay focused, I would build in this exact sequence:

1. Shared header, promo bars, footer, drawers, and chat widget.  
2. Homepage.  
3. Shop collection and one reusable product-detail template.  
4. Cart drawer and checkout connection.  
5. Results, FAQ, and free-quote form.  
6. Contact, About, Services, and Wholesale.  
7. All legal pages through the one legal-page template.  
8. Account, search, locale selector, and remaining edge states.  

This approach lets you create the **reusable commerce shell once**, then fill in high-impact content pages rather than rebuilding the same navigation and footer repeatedly. [afterfade](https://afterfade.be/en-es/policies/refund-policy)

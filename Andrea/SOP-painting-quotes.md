# SOP — Painting Service Quotes

## When to use
Use when a customer asks for a quote for Afterfade's professional on-site spray-painting service: repairing scratches, impact damage or break-in damage, or a full colour change, on aluminium, PVC and metal. This is the professional service, separate from the DIY Pro-Tech+ coating — and unlike the coating, the spray service can change the colour.

## How customers request a quote
The customer requests a free, no-obligation quote at afterfade.be/pages/offerte, or by sending photos to WhatsApp **+32 499 14 36 96** (the quote WhatsApp number). There is no fixed public price list for this service — never invent a price; direct the customer to request a quote.

## Information to collect
Ask for the following before any quote is given:
- **Pictures** of the surface(s) to be treated (sent to the quote WhatsApp number).
- **Material** — aluminium, PVC, metal, or something else.
- **What they want done** — repair scratches, impact or break-in damage, or a full colour change.
- **How much there is** — roughly how many windows, doors, gates, shutters, or the approximate surface area.
- **Condition** — how bad the damage/fading is, and whether anything has already been applied or painted.
- **Location** — where the on-site work would take place.

## Notes
- Do not give a price or promise a price range; each job is quoted individually by the Afterfade team.
- Send the collected details to the Afterfade team as a task card (see SOP — Task Management) so the quote request is tracked.
- Reply in the customer's language.

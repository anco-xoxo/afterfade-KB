## 3. Safety & Compliance (Must-Know)

This section is critical. Read it carefully and memorise the key facts.

### Hazard classification

Afterfade Pro-Tech+ causes **serious eye irritation**: **H319**, signal word **Warning**, pictogram **GHS07** (exclamation mark). That is its **only** hazard classification.

- **NOT classified as flammable** — the SDS gives it as incombustible.
- Solvent-based, so advise working in a well-ventilated space and keeping away from heat and open flames as a general precaution.
- Wear **eye protection** when applying.
- **Gloves are not required** for normal application — only recommended for prolonged or repeated skin contact.
- Keep out of reach of children.

Reference: `kb/policies/safety_compliance.md`

### Hazard wording by language

When answering in a language other than English, give the H319 hazard statement in the **standard EU CLP phrasing** for that language. Never paraphrase.

| Language | Hazard statement | Signal word |
|----------|------------------|-------------|
| Dutch | Veroorzaakt ernstige oogirritatie. | Waarschuwing. |
| French | Provoque une sévère irritation des yeux. | Attention. |
| German | Verursacht schwere Augenreizung. | Achtung. |
| Spanish | Provoca irritación ocular grave. | Atención. |
| Italian | Provoca grave irritazione oculare. | Attenzione. |
| Portuguese | Provoca irritação ocular grave. | Atenção. |
| English | Causes serious eye irritation. | Warning. |

### First aid

- **If in eyes:** Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses if present and easy to do. Continue rinsing. If eye irritation persists, get medical advice.
- **If medical advice is needed:** Have the product container or label at hand.

### SDS sharing

Only send the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) to customers who have **actually placed an order** — never to people who are merely asking or have not ordered. The SDS files are stored on the drive, not on the website.

### Storage

Store between 5 and 25 degrees Celsius in a dry place, upright, in the closed original container, away from frost and heat. Shelf life: 24 months from production date in unopened packaging.

## 5. The Application Process

This is the most common area for customer questions. Know it inside out.

### The 7-step method

| Step | What happens |
|------|-------------|
| 0. Water test | Check if surface is faded (water darkens it temporarily → coating will restore that look permanently) |
| 1. Surface cleaning | Clean with Prep+ (or soap + water as fallback). Degrease thoroughly. |
| 2. Shake & test | Shake the bottle. Test on a small hidden area first. |
| 3. Apply first coat | Thin, even layer, panel by panel. Work methodically. |
| 4. Buff | After ~10 minutes, buff with microfibre cloth. Required step. |
| 5. Second coat (optional) | 24 hours after first coat, for extra protection or coverage. |
| 6. Curing & cleanup | Let cure. Clean tools. |

### Conditions

- Temperature: 10–30°C.
- No direct sunlight during application.
- Hot climate (Spain, Italy, Portugal): work in shade, apply early morning or late afternoon.

### Coverage

About 2 doors + 10 windows, or roughly 10 square metres per bottle. Varies with surface condition — older, porous, or heavily faded surfaces absorb more. Applying too heavily uses the bottle much faster than a thin coat.

**Important:** The higher published coverage figure (double garage door + exterior door + 10–15 window frames) is marked [UNDER REVIEW]. Do not quote it until resolved.

## 6. Tools & Platforms

### Shopify (order management)

Use Shopify for:
- Looking up orders and customer details
- Viewing and issuing invoices
- Creating discount codes (for UGC incentive programme)

**Creating a discount code** (for customers who share before/after photos or testimonials):
1. Go to Shopify admin → Discounts → Create discount.
2. Set type: percentage discount, 10% off.
3. Set usage limit: 1 use per customer.
4. Set expiry: 30 days from creation.
5. Share code with customer via reply.

Reference: `Andrea/SOP-ugc.md`

**Looking up orders:**
1. Shopify admin → Orders → search by order number or customer email.
2. Check order status, payment status, shipping status.
3. For address changes: edit in Shopify first, then verify in Sendcloud. If parcel is already with carrier, changes may not be possible.

Reference: `Andrea/SOP-checking-orders.md`

### Sendcloud (shipping labels & returns)

This is the most complex tool. Learn it step by step with supervision.

**Creating a shipping label:**
1. Log in to Sendcloud.
2. Go to Shipping → Create label.
3. Select carrier (bpost or DPD).
4. Enter sender details (Afterfade BV, Schaffensesteenweg 132, 3583 Paal, Belgium).
5. Enter recipient details from order.
6. Select correct product from `Andrea/items-dashboard.md` for weight and HS code.
7. HS codes must be exactly 8 digits.
8. Generate and print label.

**Creating a return label:**
1. Sendcloud → Returns → Create return label.
2. Enter customer details.
3. Select return reason.
4. Send label to customer (email or WhatsApp).
5. Return address: Carrer Sant Joan 3, 17491 Peralada, Spain.

**Filing a shipping damage claim:**
1. Log task in Trello/Vocero.
2. Look up order in Sendcloud.
3. Go to Shipping → Claims → New claim.
4. Upload evidence: photos of damage, packaging photos, order details.
5. Deadline: 14 days from delivery.
6. Track claim status. Escalate if unresolved after 3 days.

Reference: `Andrea/SOP-returns-and-refunds.md` (Parts 2–4), `Andrea/SOP-shipment-investigation.md`, `Andrea/items-dashboard.md`

### Trello / Vocero (task tracking)

All CS tasks are tracked on the Trello board: https://trello.com/b/tJxpuJim/afterfade-cs-ops

**Rules:**
1. Log every task — no task goes untracked.

## 8. Customer Communication

### Tone

- **Helpful and direct.** Answer the question. Don't over-explain.
- **No jargon.** Use plain language the customer understands.
- **Never invent prices or make promises outside policy.** If unsure, say "let me check" and look it up.
- **Never guess.** Use the knowledge base. If the KB doesn't cover it, escalate.

### Language rules

- Reply in the language the customer writes in.
- For hazard statements, always use the **standard EU CLP phrasing** from `kb/policies/safety_compliance.md`. Never paraphrase or use machine translation.

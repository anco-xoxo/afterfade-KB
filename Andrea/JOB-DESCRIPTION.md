# Job Description: Customer Support & Operations Manager (Afterfade)

## Role Summary

You manage Afterfade's customer support and operational workflow end to end — from first customer contact through product guidance, order and shipping troubleshooting, returns and escalations — while also maintaining the internal task-tracking, knowledge and account infrastructure that makes it possible for cases to get resolved. The role is broader than "customer support agent": it is a **Customer Support & Operations** function, and it is actively transitioning from hands-on, individual case handling toward operations leadership, with VA delegation planned to free up time for strategic work.

---

## 1. Email & Customer Service Management (~40% of role)

- Monitor and manage the `hello@afterfade.be` inbox, including messages that come in via contact forms.
- Reply to customer emails, WhatsApp and web chat across product, order, shipping, returns and payment questions.
- Handle customers in multiple languages — Dutch, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and English — mirroring the language the customer writes in.
- Track and meet email SLAs (24-hour response target) and prioritise using the Eisenhower Matrix triage framework (Resolve Now / Investigate & Follow Up / Route or Automate / Close-Self-Service).
- Apologise for and follow up on delays or unanswered messages; keep the inbox at zero.
- Manage damage control on customer relationships when something has gone wrong (late deliveries, lost parcels, unanswered messages, dispute threats, confusing automated emails, marketing sent before a problem is resolved).

## 2. Product & Technical Support

- Answer product questions on aluminium/PVC suitability, surface condition, application process, coverage/quantity, and the Prep+ / Pro-Tech+ system.
- Guide customers through the water test when surface suitability is uncertain.
- Explain the three-step method (clean → coat → buff) and give detailed application instructions.
- Help customers choose between the Complete Kit, Double Kit, Pro-Tech+ Refill, Prep+ and applicator pads.
- Provide quantity/coverage estimates using only approved guide figures — never invent coverage numbers.
- Avoid unsupported claims about materials, coverage or results; escalate technical questions the knowledge base doesn't cover.

## 3. Logistics & Shipping Operations

- Manage shipments and accounts across SendCloud, bpost, DPD, PostNL, Correos, GLS and InPost.
- Check tracking, investigate delayed, missing or problematic parcels, and open carrier investigations (e.g. DPD, GLS).
- Process return labels, registered returns and reshipment/refund decisions.
- Handle EU and non-EU shipping complexity, including Canary Islands customs paperwork (commercial/pro forma invoices, HS codes, IGIC) and Azores routing via UPS.
- Negotiate with carriers on tariffs and territory coverage to close logistics/coverage gaps.
- Determine when a shipping claim or internal escalation is needed, and communicate status clearly and honestly to the customer without promising outcomes.

## 4. Returns, Refunds & Complaints

- Handle return/refund requests and explain the 14+14-day withdrawal process.
- Check return eligibility, including whether sealed chemical products (Pro-Tech+, Prep+) have had their seal broken.
- Collect order numbers, descriptions and photos for damaged, defective or wrong items.
- Escalate refund, replacement or complaint decisions that require human judgement.

## 5. Invoice & Financial Tasks

- Process invoice edits on the designated processing day (Thursdays).
- Handle SEPA bank transfer payments: share bank details, confirm receipt before shipping, reconcile with orders.
- Manage discount campaigns, billing questions and B2B invoice requests.
- Create and manage discount codes in Shopify (UGC incentives, goodwill, replacements), applying correct usage limits and expiry.

## 6. Professional / B2B Enquiries

- Handle enquiries from professionals, contractors and trade/wholesale contacts.
- Distinguish DIY product requests from the professional on-site coating/spray service.
- Collect the information needed for a proper quote (photos, material, scope, approximate area, condition, prior treatments, location) — never invent a price.
- Log and route quote requests to the team, and keep a corresponding task card so nothing is lost.

## 7. Account & System Management

- Set up and maintain carrier accounts (DPD, PostNL, Correos, bpost, InPost).
- Configure and troubleshoot core tools: Vocero, Shopify, SendCloud, Trello.
- Manage authentication, access and device setup for CS tooling.
- Identify and report platform bugs or limitations (e.g. notification noise, broken search, UI blockers) for follow-up.

## 8. Process Improvement & Documentation

- Create and maintain SOPs and operational workflows.
- Document processes and historical context in Notion; track lifecycle tasks in Trello.
- Identify gaps in the knowledge base, correct outdated information, and turn recurring questions into documented procedures.
- Flag contradictions between the website, SOPs and actual operations rather than resolving them silently.
- Build and maintain onboarding materials and training new CS hires.

## 9. Escalation & Judgement

Recognise and route, rather than guess on, situations including:
- Refund/replacement decisions and complaints
- Shipping claims requiring human intervention
- Professional/bulk/trade quotes outside the approved workflow
- Custom colour requests
- Manufacturer/sourcing questions
- Coverage calculations where available data is insufficient
- Any contradiction between sources that can't be safely resolved

## 10. VA Management (in progress / future)

- Plan and structure the VA role (target split: ~60% operations & escalations, ~40% email handling & VA management for your own role).
- Delegate appropriate tasks to the VA.
- Oversee VA performance and quality of responses.

## 11. Strategic & Administrative

- Plan and execute the transition from hands-on customer service toward an operations-focused role.
- Lead carrier negotiations (tariffs, coverage, service levels).
- Evaluate and select tools (e.g. assessing alternatives such as Zendesk).
- Monitor social media for customer-facing issues or mentions.
- Produce documentation and reference material for the wider team.

---

## Role Evolution

This role is actively shifting from **individual case handling** to **operations leadership**: less time on every single reply, more time on carrier strategy, tooling, documentation, and managing a VA — while retaining ownership of escalations, complaints and anything requiring judgement the knowledge base can't cover.

## Core Tools

Trello · Vocero · Shopify · SendCloud · Notion · WhatsApp Business · carrier portals (DPD, bpost, PostNL, Correos, InPost, GLS)

## Reporting Line / Collaboration

Works alongside the Afterfade team on escalations, refund/replacement decisions, professional quotes and carrier negotiations; coordinates VA delegation as that role is built out.

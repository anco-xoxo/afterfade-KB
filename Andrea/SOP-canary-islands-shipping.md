# SOP — Shipping to the Canary Islands

## When to use
Use when an order ships to the Canary Islands (e.g. an AF-1179 order to Yaiza). These orders need extra customs handling because the Canary Islands are outside the EU VAT/customs territory even though they are part of Spain — shipping there from Belgium is treated like an export to a non-EU country.

## In Sendcloud
Make sure the order value, product description and HS code are filled in correctly on the label — carriers prepare the export paperwork from this data. Check the customs/HS-code fields on the Canary Islands labels before shipping.

## Core documents
- **Commercial invoice or pro forma invoice** — required for essentially all non-document parcels; usually two copies. Must show the goods description, value and HS code.
- **HS code** for the goods so customs can classify them.
- **Customs / export declaration** — usually handled by the carrier (DPD, GLS, bpost, etc.) based on the invoice information provided.

## Depending on value
- **Over €150:** standard H1/SAD (Single Administrative Document) import procedure — more involved, and may trigger duties plus Spanish IGIC.
- **€150 or less:** simplified "low value shipment" / H7 procedure.
- **Shipment (not per item) over €1,000:** a full SAD is typically required even from the Belgian export side.

## Practical points
- The recipient in the Canary Islands may need to provide a Tax ID / NIF and cooperate with local customs to release the parcel.
- IGIC (the Canary Islands' local indirect tax, generally about 7%) and possibly duties apply, and are usually paid by the recipient before delivery.
- Carriers prepare the export paperwork as long as accurate invoice and product details are given when the label is created.

# SOP — Product Application (Pro-Tech+)

## When to use
Use when a customer asks how to apply Afterfade Pro-Tech+, needs help with surface preparation, or wants to know if the product will work on their surface. Covers the full workflow from initial surface check through to finished application.

---

## Step 0: Surface Check (Water Test)

Before applying Pro-Tech+, verify the surface is faded and suitable.

1. Clean a small area well, removing dirt and grease, and let it dry completely.
2. Place a few drops of clean water on the surface.
3. Observe how the water behaves:
   - If it stays in droplets on the surface, the surface is relatively non-absorbent.
   - If the water spreads into a thin film, that can also be normal on very smooth surfaces.
4. If the surface absorbs the water or visibly changes colour, contact the Afterfade team before applying the product to a larger area.
5. The test is only a preliminary check — still test Pro-Tech+ itself on a small discreet area first to confirm the result before treating the whole surface.

### What the result means
If the colour temporarily comes back when a dull part of the surface is wetted, the surface is faded and the chance of a successful result is high — and re-painting may not be needed at all.

---

## Step 1: Surface Cleaning Before Pro-Tech+

Before applying Pro-Tech+, the surface must be clean, dry and free of residue.

### Preferred method — Afterfade Prep+
1. Make sure the surface is free from loose dirt and debris.
2. Clean/degrease the surface with **Afterfade Prep+**.
3. Wipe the surface evenly, removing dirt, grease, oil and weathered film.
4. Allow the surface to **dry completely**.
5. Apply Pro-Tech+ only once the surface is clean, dry and free of residue.

Prep+ is specifically intended to prepare aluminium and PVC before applying Pro-Tech+.

### If Prep+ is not available
1. Clean the surface first with **water and soap** to remove dirt and general surface contamination.
2. Rinse/wipe away any soap residue.
3. Allow the surface to **dry completely**.
4. Then use **Prep+** as the final degreasing/preparation step.
5. Once completely dry, proceed with the Pro-Tech+ coating.

### Important
- Do not apply Pro-Tech+ to a dirty, greasy or damp surface.
- Do not skip the degreasing step: proper preparation is important for an even and durable result.
- Avoid direct sunlight while applying Prep+.
- Do not use Prep+ on a surface that has already been treated with Pro-Tech+.
- Test a small, inconspicuous area first when appropriate.

### Short customer-facing version
> First clean the surface with water and soap if it is very dirty, let it dry completely, then use Afterfade Prep+ to degrease it before applying Pro-Tech+.

## Step 2: Shake & Test
- Shake the bottle well. Use it undiluted and do not mix it with anything else.
- Test a small hidden area first, always for materials outside aluminium and PVC.

## Step 4: Apply First Coat
- Apply one thin, even coat with the microfibre applicator or a cloth, panel by panel.
- Do not overload the surface; the product should soak in rather than sit on top.

## Step 5: Buff (Required)
- After 10 minutes, wipe the surface off and polish it with a clean, dry, lint-free microfibre cloth.
- **This step is required, not optional.** Skipping it leaves a shiny, uneven finish that is slippery when wet.

## Step 6: Second Coat (Optional)
- Two thin coats are fine: apply the second coat 24 hours after the first.
- No need to degrease again in between, as long as the surface has stayed clean and dust-free.

## Step 7: Curing & Cleanup
- Keep the surface protected and dry for 24 hours afterwards.
- Clean tools and applicators with white spirit immediately after use.

### Weather conditions
Apply when both the surface and the air are between 10 and 30 degrees Celsius, out of direct sun, and not in rain or very hot weather.

**In hot climates (Spain, Italy, southern Portugal, etc.):** apply only in the morning or late afternoon, never in direct midday sun. High surface temperatures cause the product to dry too fast, preventing proper absorption and leaving streaks or an uneven finish.

---

## Coverage
The guide figure is about 2 doors plus 10 windows, or roughly 10 square metres, per bottle. Quote that figure. A higher figure is published on Afterfade's own product and kit pages and is [UNDER REVIEW], so do not repeat it. Coverage varies with surface condition: older, porous or heavily faded surfaces absorb more, a second coat needs extra, and applying too heavily uses the bottle up much faster than a thin coat does. Treat every coverage figure as a guide, never a guarantee.

```
Boa tarde Alex,

Muito obrigado pelas fotografias e pela informação adicional.

Confirmámos o seu caso com o nosso especialista e, pelas características da superfície, o Afterfade Pro-Tech+ deverá funcionar também no alumínio anodizado preto.

Como precaução, recomendamos que faça primeiro um pequeno teste numa zona discreta da caixilharia:

1. Limpe bem uma pequena área, removendo sujidade e gordura, e deixe-a secar completamente.
2. Coloque algumas gotas de água limpa sobre a superfície.
3. Observe como a água se comporta. Se ficar em gotas sobre a superfície, significa que esta é relativamente não absorvente. Se a água se espalhar numa película fina, isso também pode ser normal em superfícies muito lisas.

Este teste é apenas uma verificação preliminar e não substitui um teste de aplicação. Recomendamos também testar primeiro o próprio Pro-Tech+ numa pequena área discreta para confirmar o resultado antes de tratar toda a caixilharia.

Pelas informações e fotografias que nos enviou, o nosso especialista considera que o produto deverá ser adequado para a sua caixilharia de alumínio anodizado preto.

Com os melhores cumprimentos,

Afterfade Support
```

## Template Reply (Portuguese) — Application Steps
```
Boa tarde [Name],

Seguem as instruções de aplicação do Afterfade Pro-Tech+:

1. Limpe e desengordure a superfície com Afterfade Prep+ e deixe secar completamente. Se a superfície estiver muito suja, lave-a primeiro com água e sabão, deixe secar e só depois utilize o Prep+.
2. Agite bem o frasco. Aplique sem diluir e sem misturar com mais nada.
3. Teste primeiro numa pequena zona discreta, especialmente para materiais diferentes de alumínio e PVC.

5. Após 10 minutos, passe um pano de microfibra limpo, seco e sem fiapos para polir. Este passo é obrigatório — sem ele, a superfície fica brilhante, irregular e escorregadia quando molhada.
6. Mantenha a superfície protegida e seca durante 24 horas.
7. Limpe os materiais e aplicadores com aguarrás imediatamente após a utilização.

Condições: aplique quando a superfície e o ar estiverem entre 10 e 30°C, à sombra, sem chuva ou calor excessivo. Em países quentes como Espanha e Itália, aplique apenas de manhã ou ao fim da tarde — nunca ao sol do meio-dia, pois o calor faz o produto secar demasiado rápido.

Cobertura: aproximadamente 2 portas + 10 janelas (≈10 m²) por frasco.

Qualquer dúvida, estamos ao dispor.

Cumprimentos,
Afterfade Support
```

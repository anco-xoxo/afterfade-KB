# Afterfade Safety & Compliance

## Afterfade Pro-Tech+ hazard classification
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ causes serious eye irritation: H319, signal word Warning, pictogram GHS07, the exclamation mark. That is its only hazard classification. It is not classified as flammable — its safety data sheet gives it as incombustible, so when a customer asks "is it flammable?" the answer is no. It is solvent-based, so still advise working in a well ventilated space, keeping it away from heat and open flames as a general precaution, and wearing eye protection when applying — that is sensible practice for any solvent-based product, not a sign of a flammability classification.

## Afterfade Pro-Tech+ safe use and storage
- Keep out of reach of children.
- If medical advice is needed, have the product container or label at hand.
- Avoid contact with the eyes. Wear eye protection when applying, and gloves for prolonged or repeated skin contact.
- If in eyes: rinse cautiously with water for several minutes; remove contact lenses if present and easy to do; continue rinsing. If eye irritation persists, get medical advice.
- Store between 5 and 25 degrees Celsius in a dry place, upright, in the closed original container, away from frost and heat.
- Shelf life is up to 24 months from the production date in unopened original packaging.
- Full hazard and first-aid detail is on the product label and safety data sheet.

## SDS, MSDS and UFI terminology
- **SDS — Safety Data Sheet.** The standardized 16-section document (required under EU REACH Regulation 1907/2006, Annex II) giving the full hazard profile of a chemical product: composition, classification, first aid, handling, storage, transport, disposal and regulatory info. Aimed primarily at professional users, distributors and emergency responders rather than end consumers.
- **MSDS — Material Safety Data Sheet.** The older, pre-2010 US/international term for the same kind of document. Under the EU CLP/REACH framework the correct EU term is SDS, not MSDS — same concept, but "MSDS" is outdated terminology in an EU regulatory context. Afterfade's own documents and replies should say SDS (Dutch: veiligheidsinformatieblad), not MSDS.
- **UFI — Unique Formula Identifier.** A 16-character alphanumeric code (format XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) assigned to a specific mixture's exact formulation. It is printed on the product label and appears in the SDS, and lets poison control centres pull up the precise composition of a product instantly in an emergency (e.g. a poisoning call). Mandated under CLP Annex VIII; tied to Belgium's Antigifcentrum notification system in this case.

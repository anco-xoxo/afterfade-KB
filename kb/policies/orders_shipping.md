# Afterfade Orders & Shipping

## Where Afterfade ships
Afterfade ships from Belgium. Its current shipping area is Belgium, the Netherlands, France, Germany, Italy, Spain and Portugal. Orders are placed on afterfade.be.

## Afterfade delivery times and carriers
- Processing: Afterfade aims to ship orders within 3 business days of payment being received.
- Carriers: bpost and DPD, via Afterfade's logistics partner Sendcloud. At checkout, where available, customers can choose home delivery or a pickup point.
- Delivery time after shipment is indicative only and depends on the carrier and the destination; Afterfade does not promise a fixed delivery date.
- Tracking: customers receive a shipping confirmation with track & trace automatically once the order has shipped.

## Afterfade shipping fees
- Shipping costs are calculated and shown at checkout before the order is confirmed.
- Belgium and the Netherlands: free shipping on orders over €60.
- The rest of Europe: free shipping on orders over €130.
- Below those thresholds, a flat €5.99 shipping fee applies.
- Refunds cover the purchase price and standard shipping only, not any premium delivery surcharge.
- Risk of loss or damage passes to the customer once they (or someone they've named) physically receive the goods.

## Afterfade shipping and dangerous-goods status
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ is not classified as a dangerous good for transport: no ADR, IMDG or IATA classification and no UN number. Afterfade orders ship by standard ground (road) delivery to the countries served, with no dangerous-goods surcharge or restriction.

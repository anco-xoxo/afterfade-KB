## 1. Right of Withdrawal

As a consumer, you have the right to withdraw from your order within **14 calendar days** from receipt, without needing to justify your decision. To exercise this right, notify AFTERFADE BV within that period by email (hello@afterfade.be). After notifying, you have an additional **14 calendar days** to return the goods.

**Before delivery:** You may also withdraw before your order arrives. Notify us as soon as possible with your order number. If the package has not yet left our warehouse, we will stop it and refund the full amount. If the package is already with the carrier, we cannot recover it — in that case, please accept the delivery and return it as a registered return per sections 4 and 5. You may also refuse the package or fail to collect it. Note: a refused package returns via the carrier's return network, which from abroad can take several weeks and involve multiple carriers, and your refund will follow that timeline.

## 2. Return Conditions

To be eligible for a refund, returned goods must be:
- Complete and, where possible, in the original packaging with all supplied accessories
- In a condition that allows the nature, characteristics, and functioning of the product to be determined

You may inspect the product as you would in a physical shop. If usage exceeds what is necessary for inspection, a value reduction may be charged.

## 3. Exceptions to the Right of Withdrawal

Per Article VI.53 of the Belgian Economic Law Code, the right of withdrawal does not apply to:
- Products manufactured to consumer specifications
- Personalised products
- Sealed goods not suitable for return for health or hygiene reasons once the seal is broken
- Goods that, after delivery, become inseparably mixed with other products

For sealed treatments, cleaners, and other chemical products, the right of withdrawal is extinguished once the seal is broken.

## 4. Registering a Return

Returns must be registered before shipping by email (hello@afterfade.be), providing:
- Your name
- Order number
- Product(s) to return
- Reason (optional)

You will then receive return instructions. Unregistered returns may be processed with delays.

## 5. Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping costs are borne by the customer. Since you arrange the return, we cannot calculate the amount in advance and it may be expensive: a return from Italy or Portugal typically costs more than the original delivery. As a guide, budget at least the amount you paid for delivery, and more if the package is larger or heavier.

AFTERFADE BV only covers return shipping if:
- The wrong product was delivered
- The product arrived damaged
- The product has a manufacturing defect
- The law requires it

We never charge administrative, handling, or restocking fees.

## 7. Refunds

After receiving and inspecting the returned goods, AFTERFADE BV will process a refund of:
- The purchase price
- Standard shipping costs from the original order

Premium delivery surcharges are not refunded. Refunds are made via the original payment method, unless otherwise agreed, within **14 calendar days** of notification of withdrawal. We may withhold the refund until the goods are received or you provide proof of return shipment.

## 8. Exchanges

To exchange a product, contact hello@afterfade.be. Exchanges depend on stock availability. Price differences or additional shipping costs may apply.

## 9. Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or wrong product, contact us as soon as possible with:
- Your order number
- A clear description of the problem
- Photos of the product and packaging (if possible)

AFTERFADE BV will investigate and offer an appropriate solution such as replacement, repair, refund, or other suitable compensation.

## 10. Loss During Return Transport

We recommend using a tracked shipping method for returns. AFTERFADE BV is not responsible for returns lost or damaged during transport when the return was not organised by AFTERFADE BV.

## 11. Warranty & Protection

### Pro-Tech+ Performance (up to 5 years per coat)
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ protects for up to five years per coat. This is a performance expectation, not a separate written performance warranty — there is no additional formal guarantee on that performance figure. As a maintenance product, reapplying a light coat periodically keeps surfaces protected.

### Statutory Legal Warranty (2 years)
As with any product sold to EU consumers, Afterfade products carry the standard statutory legal warranty against defects (two years under Belgian consumer law). This covers faults present in the product itself, not normal wear, incorrect use, or the coating fading again over time.

### Damaged Orders
Report an order that arrives damaged or faulty by email, web chat, or WhatsApp. AFTERFADE BV covers the return in those cases.

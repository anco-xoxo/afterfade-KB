# Afterfade Returns & Warranty

## Afterfade returns window and how to start a return
Customers have 14 calendar days from receiving an Afterfade order to notify Afterfade they wish to return it, and a further 14 days to send the items back. To start a return, contact Afterfade by email (hello@afterfade.be) or WhatsApp (+32 499 14 36 96) with your name, order number, product details and reason. Returns that are not notified in advance may be delayed.

## Afterfade returns conditions and return-shipping costs
Returned items must be complete and, where possible, in the original packaging with all accessories. Customers may inspect a product as they would in a shop; excessive use beyond that may reduce the refund. The customer pays return postage, except when Afterfade is at fault, the wrong item was sent, the item arrived damaged, or the product is faulty. Sealed coatings and cleaners (Afterfade Pro-Tech+ and Afterfade Prep+) lose their return right once the seal is broken, and custom or personalised items cannot be returned.

## Afterfade refunds
Afterfade refunds within 14 calendar days of being notified of the return, though it may wait until the goods arrive back or proof of return shipment is provided. Refunds cover the purchase price and standard shipping, not any premium delivery surcharge. The full policy is at afterfade.be/policies/refund-policy.

## Afterfade guarantee and damaged orders
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ protects for up to five years per coat, a performance expectation, not a separate written performance warranty; there is no additional formal guarantee on that performance figure. Because it is a maintenance product, reapplying a light coat periodically keeps surfaces protected. Separately, as with any product sold to EU consumers, Afterfade products carry the standard statutory legal warranty against defects (two years under Belgian consumer law) — this covers faults present in the product itself, not normal wear, incorrect use, or the coating fading again over time. Report an order that arrives damaged or faulty by email, web chat or WhatsApp; Afterfade covers the return in those cases.

# Afterfade E-Invoicing & VAT

## Belgium's e-invoicing mandate
Since 1 January 2026, Belgium requires structured electronic invoicing for domestic B2B transactions between VAT-registered businesses established in Belgium. PDFs and other traditional invoice formats are no longer acceptable for transactions within scope — invoices must use a structured, machine-readable format.

Key details:
- **Who's affected:** All VAT-registered businesses in Belgium, for domestic B2B transactions. This builds on the B2G (business-to-government) e-invoicing rules that were already in place.
- **Format/network:** Invoices must run on the Peppol 4-corner network, in a format compliant with the EN 16931 standard (either UBL 2.1 or CII 16B). In practice most businesses use Peppol BIS 3.0.
- **Grace period:** The tax authority (SPF Finances) announced it will not impose penalties for infractions during the first three months of 2026 — but this tolerance only applies if the business can show it made reasonable, timely efforts to comply (e.g. already had a Peppol access point in progress). It is not a blanket delay.
- **Penalties (after tolerance ends):** Graduated fines starting from €1,500 for non-compliance.
- **Archiving:** Invoices must be kept for 7 years.

What is coming next:
- **January 2028** — real-time/near-real-time e-reporting to the tax authority kicks in (replacing the periodic customer listing system), moving to a Peppol 5-corner model.
- **July 2030** — aligns with the EU-wide ViDA (VAT in the Digital Age) e-reporting requirement for cross-border intra-EU B2B transactions.

In short: if Afterfade invoices another VAT-registered business in Belgium, it must now send and receive that invoice as a structured e-invoice over Peppol rather than as a PDF or paper — and real-time reporting to the tax authority is the next phase, arriving in 2028.

## How to say VAT in other countries
Customers may use a local abbreviation for VAT. Use the customer's own local term where possible.

| Market | Local abbreviation | Full local name | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Netherlands, Belgium (Dutch) | BTW | Belasting Toegevoegde Waarde | Value Added Tax |
| Belgium (French), France | TVA | Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée | Value Added Tax |
| Germany | USt | Umsatzsteuer | Value Added Tax |
| Spain | IVA | Impuesto sobre el Valor Añadido | Value Added Tax |
| Italy | IVA | Imposta sul Valore Aggiunto | Value Added Tax |
| Portugal | IVA | Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado | Value Added Tax |
| English-speaking markets | VAT | Value Added Tax | Value Added Tax |

Note: sales tax in the United States is not VAT; US states collect their own sales taxes and the federal government has no VAT.

# Afterfade, Company Overview

## About Afterfade
Afterfade sells DIY restoration coatings that bring faded aluminium and PVC back to their original colour and gloss, window frames, doors, gates, garage doors and shutters. Customers apply the products themselves, in an afternoon, without a contractor or renovation. Afterfade BV is based in Paal (Beringen), Belgium, and sells across the EU through afterfade.be. Afterfade also offers a professional on-site restoration service (see Coatings Overview) and a trade/wholesale partner programme (see the contact section below).

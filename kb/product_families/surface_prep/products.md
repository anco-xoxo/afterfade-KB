# Afterfade Surface Prep, Products

## Afterfade Prep+, Surface Degreaser, 500 ml
- Purpose: cleans and degreases aluminium and PVC before coating; removes grease, dirt, oil, fingerprints and weathered film. Also usable as a stand-alone deep cleaner for aluminium and PVC.
- Base: water-based, biodegradable, residue-free, streak-free on smooth surfaces.
- Application: mist Afterfade Prep+ onto a clean, dry microfibre cloth (rather than directly onto the surface) until evenly damp — this gives a more even spread. Wipe the surface with the damp cloth, keeping even pressure across the whole area; do not scrub one spot harder than the rest, as concentrated rubbing can damage the surface. A second pass may be needed on a heavily soiled surface. Test on a small, inconspicuous area first. Never use Afterfade Prep+ on a surface already treated with Afterfade Pro-Tech+. Avoid direct sunlight while applying. Let the surface dry completely before applying Afterfade Pro-Tech+.
- Format: trigger-spray bottle.
- Sizes: sold stand-alone as 500 ml, SKU AF-PREP-500. Also supplied inside the Complete Kit (500 ml) and the Double Kit (2 × 500 ml) — see Kits.

# Afterfade Surface Prep, Overview

## What Afterfade Prep+ is
Afterfade Prep+ (Degreaser Prep+) is a water-based cleaner and degreaser used to prepare aluminium and PVC before applying the Afterfade Pro-Tech+ coating. It removes the grease, dirt, oil, fingerprints and weathered surface film that stop a coating bonding. It is biodegradable and leaves no residue. Prep+ is step 1 of the Afterfade method; Pro-Tech+ is step 2.

## Why Afterfade Prep+ matters
Skipping degreasing is the most common reason an Afterfade coating does not last or looks patchy. Afterfade Prep+ leaves a clean, sound, residue-free surface for the Pro-Tech+ coating to bond to. It can also be used on its own as a deep cleaner for aluminium and PVC frames, doors, shutters and garden furniture. After cleaning, the surface must be fully dry before Afterfade Pro-Tech+ is applied.

# Afterfade Kits, Products

## Afterfade Complete Kit: Restoration Kit (1 x Kit)
- SKU: AF-KIT-HOME-1. Price: €79,95.
- Contents: 250 ml Afterfade Pro-Tech+ coating, 500 ml Afterfade Prep+ degreaser, three 40 x 40 cm microfibre cloths, one applicator pad (125 mm).
- Restores aluminium and PVC: window frames, doors, gates, garage doors, shutters. Suitable for any job size, from a single feature to a whole house — buy two kits (or the Double Kit) for a bigger job.
- Coverage guide: see Coatings coverage guidance (about 2 doors plus 10 windows, or roughly 10 square metres, from the coating in one kit). Afterfade's own kit listing states a higher figure (a double garage door, an exterior door and 10-15 window frames) — that figure is [UNDER REVIEW]; do not quote it.
- Protection: up to 5 years against sun and UV.
- Position: best value in the range; the recommended starting point for a first-time customer.

## Afterfade Complete Kit, 2 x Kits option
- SKU: AF-KIT-HOME-2. Price: €134,95 (two kits bought together).

- This is the same combination, at the same price, as the separately-listed Afterfade Double Kit below — recommend whichever the customer is already looking at.

## Afterfade Double Kit
- SKU: AF-KIT-DOUBLE. Price: €134,95.
- Contents: 2 x 250 ml Afterfade Pro-Tech+ coating, 2 x 500 ml Afterfade Prep+ degreaser, 6 microfibre cloths (40 x 40 cm), 2 applicator pads (125 mm) — two complete kits' worth.
- Positioning: built for a whole house — a full set of windows plus a front door and a garage door usually needs more than one kit. Afterfade's own listing frames the saving as "two kits bought separately would be €159,90, so the bundle saves €24,95."
- Protection: up to 5 years against sun and UV.

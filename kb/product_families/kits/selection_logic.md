# Afterfade Kits, Selection Logic

## Which Afterfade kit for the job
- Afterfade currently sells one kit size, the Afterfade Complete Kit (250 ml coating, 500 ml degreaser, 3 cloths, 1 applicator pad) — recommend it for any first-time customer's job, whether that is a single feature (one gate, one garage door) or a whole house.
- Whole house, or several features (windows plus doors plus garage) → two Afterfade Complete Kits, or the equivalent Afterfade Double Kit (same combined contents, same €134,95 total).
- One feature only, a garage door, a gate, a front door, or a small run of doors → a single Afterfade Complete Kit is normally enough; if the single feature is unusually large (for example a double garage door alone), mention that the guide coverage is about 2 doors plus 10 windows per 250 ml bottle, so a large single feature can use most of a kit's coating.
- If a customer asks by name for a "Gate & Door Kit", Afterfade has no product by that name. Do not take an order for it, and do not say it was discontinued or ever sold — simply say we don't have a product by that name and recommend the Complete Kit (or the Double Kit for a bigger job), which covers single-feature jobs as well as whole-house jobs.
- Coverage depends on surface condition: older, porous or heavily faded aluminium and PVC absorbs more, and a second coat needs extra, if in doubt, size up.

## Afterfade kits versus buying separately
- A kit includes the Afterfade Prep+ degreaser and applicators; the stand-alone Pro-Tech+ bottle does not.
- Returning customer who already has Afterfade Prep+ and applicators → the stand-alone Afterfade Pro-Tech+ 250 ml bottle (also listed as "Pro-Tech+ Refill"), not a kit — there is no separate, different refill product.
- Customer who only needs to clean, not coat → Afterfade Prep+ on its own.
- Customer who just needs more cloths or applicator pads (not more coating or degreaser) → the accessories: the microfibre cloth, the applicator pad, or the cloth-and-pad bundle. See Kits, Products.

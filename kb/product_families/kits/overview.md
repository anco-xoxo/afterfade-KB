# Afterfade Kits, Overview

## What the Afterfade kits are
Afterfade sells the Afterfade Complete Kit, which combines the Prep+ degreaser, the Pro-Tech+ coating and the applicators needed to do a job in one box. It is Afterfade's one kit size and the recommended starting point for a first-time customer, of any job size, because it includes everything needed to clean and coat. For a bigger job, a customer can buy two Complete Kits (a "2 x Kits" option at the same price as the separately-listed Afterfade Double Kit, below) rather than sizing up to a different product. Both restore the original colour and gloss and protect for up to five years.

## Afterfade Double Kit
The Afterfade Double Kit is two complete kits' worth of contents (coating, degreaser and applicators, doubled) in one listing, at the same price as buying "2 x Kits" of the Complete Kit. It exists as its own product page as well as being offered as a quantity option on the Complete Kit — both lead to the same outcome. Recommend whichever the customer finds first; there is no difference in contents or price.

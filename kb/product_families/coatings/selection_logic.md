# Afterfade Coatings, Selection Logic

## Choosing between the Afterfade Pro-Tech+ bottle and a kit
- First-time customer with a surface to restore and no degreaser or applicator, of any job size → recommend the Afterfade Complete Kit; it includes Afterfade Prep+ and the applicators alongside the coating. Afterfade currently sells one kit size (with a 2-kit option for bigger jobs — see Kits).
- Customer who already owns Afterfade Prep+ and applicators, or is topping up for maintenance → the stand-alone Afterfade Pro-Tech+ 250 ml bottle (EUR 59,95, also listed as "Pro-Tech+ Refill"). There is no separate, different refill product; the standard bottle is the re-buy.
- Amount needed: the guide figure is about 10 square metres per bottle, or 2 doors plus 10 windows. For a larger job (a whole house, or several features) recommend two Complete Kits, or the Afterfade Double Kit (same combined contents, one listing). If a customer describes a large job, size up rather than promising it will stretch.

## When Afterfade Pro-Tech+ is not the right product
- Surface is glass → not suitable. Surface is a floor, deck, step or path, or is in prolonged contact with water → not suitable, do not recommend it. Surface is stone, plastic, painted metal, painted wood or another material outside aluminium and PVC → Pro-Tech+ can still be used, so do not refuse; explain that Afterfade's guidance covers aluminium and PVC and that the customer should test a small hidden area first. Bare or untreated wood is never suitable — only painted or previously coated wood. See Surfaces.
- Customer wants to change the colour or match a new shade → not possible; Afterfade Pro-Tech+ is clear and restores the existing colour only.
- Customer wants to waterproof a terrace, driveway or path → out of scope; these are walking surfaces and Afterfade does not recommend Pro-Tech+ for them.

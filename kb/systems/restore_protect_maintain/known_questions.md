# Afterfade Known Questions

Typical user questions:
- Will this work on my faded aluminium window frames?
- Does it work on PVC/plastic shutters?
- Is this a paint? Will it change the colour?
- How long does it really last?
- How much do I need to do my whole house?
- Which kit should I buy, I only want to do my garage door?
- Do I have to clean the surface first?
- Can I use it on my wooden door, on glass, or on my stone terrace?
- Can I apply it in the sun?
- How long before it can rain or get wet? (Answer: keep it protected and dry for 24 hours.)
- Do I have to wipe it off afterwards? (Answer: yes, after 10 minutes, and polish with a dry lint-free microfibre cloth.)
- Can I use it on my garden furniture, my car, my wooden gate?
- Is it safe? Is it flammable?
- Can I get more coating without buying the whole kit again?

Agent notes:
- Surfaces: Afterfade sells and supports Pro-Tech+ for aluminium and PVC, and that is what to recommend first. Pro-Tech+ can also be used on plastic, painted surfaces, metal, painted wood and stone, so do not tell a customer it will not work on those; say it can be used on the material, that Afterfade's guidance covers aluminium and PVC, and that they should test a hidden area first. Wood is only suitable if painted or previously coated — never recommend it for bare or untreated wood, and never promise a lifespan on wood. Never recommend it for glass, for floors, decking, steps or paths, or for anything in prolonged contact with water. See Surfaces.
- Claims discipline: say "up to five years" and "restores the original colour and gloss". Never say "permanent", "lifetime" or "like new windows". It is clear and does not recolour, do not offer colour choices.
- Coverage is a guide, not a promise: the guide figure is about 10 square metres, or 2 doors plus 10 windows, per bottle. Use that figure. The higher figure in some Afterfade marketing is under review, do not repeat it. Older, porous or heavily faded surfaces use more. Never guarantee an exact area.
- Safety: Pro-Tech+ causes serious eye irritation (H319, signal word Warning, GHS07) — that is its only hazard classification. It is NOT classified as flammable (its safety data sheet gives it as incombustible), so "is it flammable?" is answered no. Still advise a ventilated workspace and eye protection, and point to the label/SDS; never downplay the eye hazard. Prep+ safety is pending its SDS, do not call Prep+ non-hazardous yet.
- White-label rule: Afterfade is the only voice in every reply. Never name who produces the product, any factory or production partner, or any internal product code. If a customer asks who makes it, say it is produced for Afterfade to its specification and move on. Never attribute facts, figures or scope to an unnamed outside party either (no "the people who make it say..." phrasing) — state everything as Afterfade's own guidance.
- Ask a clarifying question when the surface material is unclear (aluminium/PVC vs painted wood vs bare wood) before recommending — bare wood is never suitable.
- Kits: Afterfade currently sells one kit size, the Complete Kit (with a 2-kit / Double Kit option for bigger jobs). If a customer asks by name for a "Gate & Door Kit", Afterfade has no product by that name — never take an order for it, and never say it was discontinued or ever sold. Simply say we don't have a product by that name and recommend the Complete Kit (or the Double Kit for a bigger job); never volunteer any history about it.
- Merchandise: Afterfade also sells an AfterFade T-Shirt. It is apparel, not a coating product — if asked, confirm it exists rather than denying it.
- Escalate to a human: bulk/trade orders beyond the wholesale partner form, complaints or refunds, custom-colour requests, and any stone/masonry/roof sealing question.
- Language: mirror the customer's language and switch if they switch, even mid-conversation — never default to English just because the knowledge is written in English. Never translate a product name yourself or improvise a hazard statement in another language — see Company/Languages and Safety & Compliance for the pinned wording.

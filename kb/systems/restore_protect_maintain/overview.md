# Afterfade System, Restore, Protect, Maintain

## The Afterfade three-step method
Afterfade works as a three-step method:
1. Clean and prep, degrease the surface with Afterfade Prep+ and let it dry fully.
2. Restore and protect, shake the bottle, apply one thin, even coat of Afterfade Pro-Tech+ with the microfibre applicator or a cloth, panel by panel, without overloading the surface. After 10 minutes, wipe the surface off and polish it with a clean, dry, lint-free microfibre cloth. The wipe-off is part of the method, not optional. Then keep it protected and dry for 24 hours.
3. Maintain, reapply a light Pro-Tech+ coat periodically, sooner on sun-facing surfaces. A new coat goes straight onto the cleaned surface with no stripping. A further Pro-Tech+ 250 ml bottle (EUR 59,95, also listed as "Pro-Tech+ Refill") is the maintenance re-buy — there is no separate, different refill product.
The Afterfade Complete Kit and the Afterfade Double Kit each contain everything needed for steps 1 and 2 in one box.

## Conditions for applying the Afterfade system
- Apply only to a clean, degreased, fully dry surface, free of dust and grease.
- Surface and air temperature must be between 10 and 30 degrees Celsius. Work out of direct sun and away from hot surfaces, and do not apply in rain or in very hot weather.
- Use the product undiluted and do not mix it with anything else. Shake before use.
- Wipe off and polish 10 minutes after applying. If this is skipped the finish can look shiny and uneven, and the surface can become slippery when wet.
- Keep the surface protected and dry for 24 hours after application.
- Test a small hidden area first, always for materials outside aluminium and PVC.
- Mask or protect anything nearby that should not be treated, and clean up spills immediately. Clean tools with white spirit straight after use.
- Afterfade Pro-Tech+ causes serious eye irritation: wear eye protection, avoid splashes, and work in a well ventilated space. It is not classified as flammable. See Safety & Compliance.

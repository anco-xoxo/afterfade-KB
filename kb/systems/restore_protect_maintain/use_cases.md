# Afterfade System, Use Cases

## Afterfade on window frames
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ restores faded aluminium and PVC window frames to their original colour and gloss. The guide figure is about 10 windows plus 2 doors per bottle. Degrease each frame with Afterfade Prep+, let it dry, apply a thin coat, then wipe off and polish after 10 minutes. Frames only, never the glass.

## Afterfade on garage doors
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ restores faded aluminium and PVC garage doors. A double garage door is roughly 10 square metres, so on the guide figure it uses most of a full bottle on its own. Clean with Afterfade Prep+ first, apply thinly, then wipe off and polish after 10 minutes. For a garage door plus other features, the Afterfade Complete Kit is the right starting point (two kits, or the Double Kit, for a whole house).

## Afterfade on gates and railings
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ restores faded aluminium and PVC gates, driveway gates and railings. Degrease with Afterfade Prep+ first, apply thinly, then wipe off and polish after 10 minutes. A gate plus a couple of doors fits within a single Afterfade Complete Kit. Wooden gates can also be treated, but only if painted or previously coated — never bare or untreated wood — so always test a small hidden section first, and don't promise a specific lifespan on wood.

## Afterfade on shutters (persianas, volets, estores)
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ restores faded aluminium and PVC roller and slatted shutters, a common faded surface in sunny markets. Degrease with Afterfade Prep+, dry, apply a thin coat, then wipe off and polish after 10 minutes. Wooden shutters can also be treated, but only if painted or previously coated — never bare or untreated wood. This sits outside Afterfade's own tested range, so advise a test patch on a small hidden section first and do not promise a result.

## Afterfade on exterior doors
Afterfade Pro-Tech+ restores faded aluminium, PVC and composite exterior and front doors, including the aluminium/PVC frame profile of glass-wall or glazed-door systems. The guide figure is 2 doors plus 10 windows per bottle. Clean with Afterfade Prep+ before coating, then wipe off and polish after 10 minutes. Never apply Afterfade Pro-Tech+ to the glass itself, in a glazed panel or a glass wall — frame only, always. Wooden doors can also be treated, but only if painted or previously coated — never bare or untreated wood — so advise a test patch on a small hidden section first and do not promise a result.

## Afterfade on garden and outdoor furniture
Pro-Tech+ can also be used on plastic, resin, painted metal and painted wooden furniture — never bare or untreated wood. Faded plastic and resin garden furniture is a good fit for the product and a common customer question. Clean and degrease first, apply thinly, wipe off and polish after 10 minutes. This is outside Afterfade's own tested range, so always advise a test patch on a hidden area and do not promise the five-year figure. Do not treat table tops that food is prepared on, and do not treat seats before the 24-hour drying period is over.

## Afterfade on vehicles and other plastic items
Pro-Tech+ can also be used on car bodies and bumpers, and on plastic accessories generally, which extends to trim, mirror housings, planters, bins and plastic garden items. The same rules apply: clean, thin coat, wipe off and polish after 10 minutes, test patch first, no promises on lifespan. Never use it on glass, including windscreens and headlight lenses, and never on any surface that is walked on.

## Afterfade on walls and render
Pro-Tech+ can also be used on walls and stone, and it gives a water and stain repellent beading effect. Afterfade does not market or size the product for masonry, and coverage on porous render is much lower than on frames. Treat wall enquiries as possible but unsupported: say it can be used on the material, that Afterfade does not sell it as a masonry treatment, and that they should test first. Never recommend it for terraces, paths, driveways or any walking surface, because of the slip risk.
